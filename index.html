Reten privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

Reten helps you see and limit how many Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts you watch. It works entirely on your phone. This policy explains what it uses and why. The same text is in the app, under Privacy policy on the home screen.

Everything stays on your phone

Reten has no internet access: Android doesn't let it connect to anything. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no code that sends data anywhere. Nothing it stores ever leaves your phone, and no one, including the developer, can see it.

What Reten keeps

How many Reels and Shorts you watched each day; your daily limit; the apps you chose for Focus Lock and when a lock ends; and your settings. It doesn't keep what you watched, who posted it, or anything you type.

How Reten uses Accessibility

Only after you agree in the app and switch it on in Android's settings, Reten uses Android's Accessibility Service to:

  • tell when a new Reel or Short fills your screen in Instagram or YouTube, by looking at the screen's layout, so it can count it;
  • press Back to leave Reels or Shorts once you reach the daily limit you set;
  • during a Focus Lock you start, check which app is open, so it can block the apps you chose.

It reads nothing else, never acts on Settings, and doesn't record what's on screen. You can switch it off at any time in Android's Accessibility settings.

The count over Instagram and YouTube

After each video, Reten shows today's count at the top of the screen, with a short check-in every tenth video and near your limit. It's drawn by Reten on your phone and is never sent anywhere.

Paying for Pro

Pro is sold through Google Play, as a subscription or a one-time purchase, and Google Play handles your payment under Google's own privacy policy. Reten never sees your card or payment details. It only asks Google Play, through the Play Store app on your phone, whether you have Pro, and keeps that answer on this phone.

Deleting your data

Uninstall Reten, or clear its storage in Android's settings, and everything it kept is deleted. It's never included in cloud backups; moving to a new phone by cable or direct transfer can bring it along.

Children

Reten isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on its Google Play page, with a new date. Reten is made by Niraj Vishwakarma. For questions about this policy or your data, email nirajvishwa27@gmail.com.

Reten is not affiliated with Instagram, Meta, YouTube or Google.
